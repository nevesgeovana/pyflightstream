# PFS-2127.001: hover reconstruction and reference speed

Date: 2026-10-16. Owner judgment applied to licensed batch-9 observations.

Retain dimensional reconstruction using reference dynamic pressure
qref = rho VREF squared / 2. A static free stream with positive VREF can
therefore produce dimensional forces and moments and rotor CT, CQ and CP.
Zero free stream sets advance ratio J to zero; the existing efficiency
products remain not applicable. A zero reference velocity cannot recover
dimensional loads from the exported coefficients.

In batch 9, hover arms H20, H40 and H80 report Cx -1.2658094, -0.4986295
and -0.1257467. H40/H80 is 3.965, near the factor four expected for
VREF-squared normalization. Reconstructed force magnitudes are approximately
3101, 4887 and 4929 N. The reference speed also sets Reynolds number:
2.74e6, 5.48e6 and 1.10e7. Changing VREF in hover can therefore change the
solution itself; H20 is not a pure rescaling of H40. The free-stream printout
is 0.010 m/s and all arms reached 300 iterations. The original analysis
called the comparison confounded; the owner's 2026-10-16 launch instruction
settles the normalization interpretation while retaining those limitations.
