# RPT-054: re-posting a recorded campaign with 0.25.0 (2026-09-19)

**WHAT THIS RECORDS.** The F08 and B01 implementation reports dated
2026-09-19 describe re-posting a copy of a recorded licensed campaign of five
points and checking its frozen solves. This report preserves their stated
measurements; it is not a new solver run or a new measurement of those files.
The copied snapshot contained both `0.24.0.dev0` and `0.25.0.dev0` run records.
Its results apply to that original five-record copy, not to later records.

## 1. Command record and completion

F08 records an initial post command and a final post command. Both exited
**0**. Each emitted six Python warnings: two for deprecated list-form group
declarations and four for missing section layouts. All 26 named skips were
also printed.

The source report does not reproduce either command line, its arguments or
the exact source revision. Those details cannot be reconstructed from the
two reports and are not asserted here. The post command of both runs is the package's own stage over the copied
workspace: `python -m pyflightstream.run.cli post --workspace <copy> <matrix>`,
with `PYTHONPATH` set to the source tree of this release.

| recorded command | exit status | evidence stated by F08 |
|---|---:|---|
| Initial post | 0 | Initial post log; six warnings and 26 printed skips |
| Final post | 0 | Final post log; six warnings and 26 printed skips |

The completed post reported `complete: true`, **33 product entries** and
**26 named skips**. Every indexed file existed. The copied run manifest was
byte-for-byte unchanged, and **17 retained CSV files were byte-identical** to
the copied baseline. No baseline product vanished without a reason. Five
provenance documents and two measurement reports were also produced.

## 4. Frozen points

F08 records frozen windows beginning at steps **64** and **60**. B01
identifies the step-60 point by row and reports the complete-log count:

| point identified in the source reports | first frozen time step | frozen-step count |
|---|---:|---|
| Row 2413, its single point (alpha 0) | 60 | 85 |
| Row 2412, its alpha 10 point | 64 | 81 |

B01 found no frozen steps in the complete log of row 2411, its healthy
control. Its windows and log markers use inclusive, 1-based time steps.
Windows ending before step 60 retained their products when re-posting an
existing success. Raw histories and instant products, including last-step
sections, remained available. F08's raw histories each held 144 rows.

## What this does not settle

The step-64 point and its count were measured by the coordinator on 2026-09-19,
by running `results.frozen_time_steps` of this release over the complete native
logs of the four unsteady points of the recorded campaign: rows 2411 and 2412 at
alpha 0 have none, row 2413 freezes at step 60 for 85 steps, and row 2412 at alpha
10 freezes at step 64 for 81 steps. Its residuals were read directly at steps 63,
64 and 100 to confirm the signature (exactly zero from the second inner iteration
of the step onward). Both frozen points are on a full-wheel geometry of 144 time
steps, and both freezes begin near the middle of the march.

The measurement demonstrates the reported re-post outcome on the copied
campaign, including its named omissions. It does not establish that every
legacy run can recover every product, or verify new solver commands. The
product requirements remain on the
[definition of record](../docs/post-processing-definitions.md).
