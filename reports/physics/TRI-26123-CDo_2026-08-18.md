## Question

The 26.123 Tier 3 run flagged two WARNs, and both are `CDo`:

* `PHY-26123_2026-08-17_full` measures 36 pass and 2 warn against the
  stored references, which are seeded on 26.120.
* `DRF-26122-26123_2026-08-17_full` measures 36 pass and 2 warn against a
  26.122 run made the same day, with the same executables and the same
  case set.

Both name SMI-01 and SMI-02. Solver change, case-setup sensitivity, or
noise?

the source is named per column rather than per table.

Two properties survive that table and one does not.

**SURVIVES: every `CDo` in the matrix moved, and all three moved DOWN.**
The three are the only viscous drag coefficients the matrix carries, and
their relative movements are -2.22, -1.77 and -8.99 percent. PHY-05 is
synthetic geometry with no research file behind it, so the direction is
shared across geometries that have nothing else in common.

**SURVIVES: the only two out-of-band metrics are the same coefficient,
and both sit below their fail bands.** SMI-01 is 0.29 of its fail band,
SMI-02 is 0.89 of its.

**WITHDRAWN: "a case-setup or mesh difference would not confine itself to
one coefficient."** The first version of this note reasoned from that,
and the movement is not confined to one coefficient. On both SMI cases
exactly two metrics moved, `CDo` and `CMy`; on PHY-05 all four did. The
inference that excluded case setup and mesh as causes therefore has no
premise here, and nothing in this note replaces it. Excluding those
causes needs the input-identity and boundary-localisation steps the 2026
07-21 triage ran, and neither was run.

## What is NOT established, stated because the shape invites assuming it

**No rerun was made.** The 2026-07-21 triage of the SMI-01 pitching
moment excluded noise by re-running each version once and showing both
reruns bit-identical to the first measurement. That has not been done
here, and doing it costs a licensed seat. What replaces it in part, and
only in part, is that the movement is measured TWICE by two different
comparisons: against a 26.120-seeded reference and against a 26.122 run
of the same day. Two agreeing measurements are not a repeatability test.

**No cause is established.** With the confinement argument withdrawn,
what remains is a pattern consistent with a deterministic change in the
viscous drag model between builds 8092026 and 8112026, and consistent is
not the same as shown.

**It is not attributed to the boundary layer.** `RPT-027` measured a
large change in integrated viscous drag across a proximity gap on 26.123,
which the vendor attributes to the boundary-layer model; that report
refuses both the attribution and any claim of improvement in as many
words, and its corroboration section observes only that the drift run
"agrees" in DIRECTION. Its Result 2 establishes the viscous drag
coefficients move on cases with no proximity gap anywhere in them, which
excludes the mapping as the cause for exactly these two. A reader taking
that section as the disposition would be taking an attribution the report
itself rules out, and this paragraph exists so that reading is not
available.

## Decision (the author's, taken 2026-08-18)

**The references stay untouched and the WARNs stand.** No band is
widened and no reference is re-seeded on 26.123.

The decision was taken on the first version of this note and is
unchanged by the amendment, because it was the least-committing of the
three options on offer and the amendment makes the case for the other two
weaker rather than stronger: with no cause established, re-seeding would
assert one.

The stored references encode 26.120 behaviour deliberately, and a WARN
against them is the drift class doing its work rather than failing at it,
which is the same conclusion `TRI-SMI01-CMy_2026-07-21` reached about a
different coefficient on a different build pair.

## What this leaves open

**The rerun** is owed the next time a licensed seat is spent on this
build, and is registered as `PLN-20260818-0200-cdo-rerun-owed-on-26123`
and in the licensed evidence queue on the roadmap page.

**And a question the withdrawn claim had hidden, which belongs to the
numerical-analyst seat.** SMI-01's `CMy` moved by 0.70 of its warn band
on this build pair, and `CMy` on SMI-01 is precisely the coefficient
`TRI-SMI01-CMy_2026-07-21` was written about, on the 26.101 against
26.120 pair, where it moved 0.8 percent and was dispositioned as a
deterministic solver-side change. It has now moved again, on a different
pair, in the opposite direction, at seventy percent of the band that
would flag it. Whether a viscous-model-only reading is still the one the
author wants on the record is hers, and the rerun above is the
measurement that would settle it.
