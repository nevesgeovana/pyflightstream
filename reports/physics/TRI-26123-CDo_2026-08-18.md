# TRI-26123-CDo: triage of the two viscous-drag warns on 26.123 (2026-08-18)

## Question

The 26.123 Tier 3 run flagged two WARNs, and both are `CDo`:

* `PHY-26123_2026-08-17_full` measures 36 pass and 2 warn against the
  stored references, which are seeded on 26.120.
* `DRF-26122-26123_2026-08-17_full` measures 36 pass and 2 warn against a
  26.122 run made the same day, with the same executables and the same
  case set.

Both name SMI-01 and SMI-02, and no other metric of any case moved out of
band. Solver change, case-setup sensitivity, or noise?

## What the committed evidence already says

The three viscous drag coefficients in the matrix, all from
`DRF-26122-26123_2026-08-17_full.yaml`:

| case | 26.122 | 26.123 | delta | relative | warn / fail band | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SMI-01 | 0.13050 | 0.12760 | -0.00290 | -2.22 percent | 0.002 / 0.01 (abs) | warn |
| SMI-02 | 0.42290 | 0.41540 | -0.00750 | -1.77 percent | 0.005 / 0.02 (rel) | warn |
| PHY-05 | 0.00070 | 0.00064 | -0.00006 | -8.99 percent | (inside band) | pass |

Three properties of that table are what this note rests on, and each is
read from a committed report rather than asserted here.

**Only `CDo` moved.** Every other metric of both SMI cases is identical
to the fifth decimal across the two builds: SMI-01 keeps CL -0.01740,
CDi 0.00300 and CMy -1.78080 to -1.78430, and SMI-02 keeps CL 12.13680,
CDi 0.22440 and CMy -116.69210. A case-setup difference or a mesh
difference would not confine itself to one coefficient.

**Every `CDo` in the matrix moved, and all three moved DOWN.** PHY-05 is
synthetic geometry with no research file behind it and it moved by the
largest relative amount of the three; it stayed inside its band only
because its absolute value is four orders of magnitude smaller. The
common factor is the coefficient, not the geometry.

**Both exceedances are below their FAIL bands.** SMI-01 is 1.45 times its
warn band and 0.29 of its fail band; SMI-02 is 3.55 times its warn band
and 0.89 of its fail band.

## What is NOT established, stated because the shape invites assuming it

**No rerun was made.** The 2026-07-21 triage of the SMI-01 pitching
moment excluded noise by re-running each version once and showing both
reruns bit-identical to the first measurement. That has not been done
here, and doing it costs a licensed seat. What replaces it in part, and
only in part, is that the movement is measured TWICE by two different
comparisons: against a 26.120-seeded reference and against a 26.122 run
of the same day. Two agreeing measurements are not a repeatability test.

**It is not attributed to the boundary layer.** `RPT-027` measured a
boundary-layer improvement across a proximity gap on 26.123 and its
corroboration section observes that the drift run "agrees". What agrees
is the DIRECTION. That same report's Result 2 establishes the viscous
drag coefficients move on cases with no proximity gap anywhere in them,
which excludes the mapping as the cause for exactly these two. A reader
taking the corroboration section as the disposition would be taking an
attribution the report itself rules out, and this paragraph exists so
that reading is not available.

## Decision (the author's, taken 2026-08-18)

**The references stay untouched and the WARNs stand.** No band is
widened and no reference is re-seeded on 26.123.

Two reasons, and the second is the one that decides it.

The stored references encode 26.120 behaviour deliberately, and a WARN
against them is the drift class doing its work rather than failing at it,
which is the same conclusion `TRI-SMI01-CMy_2026-07-21` reached about a
different coefficient on a different build pair.

And re-seeding would assert a solver-side cause that the evidence above
does not establish. Every property measured is consistent with a
deterministic change in the viscous drag model between builds 7262026 and
its successor, and consistent is not the same as shown; the measurement
that would show it is a rerun of both builds, which is a licensed-day
item rather than a desk one.

## What this leaves open

The rerun is owed the next time a licensed seat is spent on this build.
Until then the two rows carry a recorded disposition rather than
silence, which is what this note is for: a WARN with no committed triage
was the residual the 0.8.0 review round found, and the class precedent
for closing it is `TRI-SMI01-CMy_2026-07-21`.
