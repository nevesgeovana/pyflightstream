## Question

The 26.123 Tier 3 run flagged two WARNs, and both are `CDo`:

* `PHY-26123_2026-08-17_full` measures 36 pass and 2 warn against the
  stored references, which are seeded on 26.120.
* `DRF-26122-26123_2026-08-17_full` measures 36 pass and 2 warn against a
  26.122 run made the same day, with the same executables and the same
  case set.

Both name SMI-01 and SMI-02. Solver change, case-setup sensitivity, or
noise?

Two properties survive that table and one does not.

**SURVIVES, with its scope corrected: every `CDo` METRIC moved, and all
three moved DOWN.** Those three are the band-judged viscous drag
coefficients, and their relative movements are -2.22, -1.77 and -8.99
percent. PHY-05 is synthetic geometry with no research file behind it, so
the direction is shared across geometries that have nothing else in
common.

The word METRIC is doing work and the first two versions of this note
left it out. Every case reports a `CDo` in its point totals, and most of
those did NOT move: comparing `PHY-26122_2026-08-11` against
`PHY-26123_2026-08-17_full`, PHY-01 is unchanged at a0, a2 and a4
(0.0067, 0.0070, 0.0073) and moved at a6 (0.0081 to 0.0080), and PHY-02's
two points are unchanged at 0.0073. One unreported `CDo` therefore moved,
downward, and several did not move at all. That comparison is against the
2026-08-11 run rather than a same-day one, so it is corroboration of
direction and not a fourth measurement.

**SURVIVES: the only two out-of-band metrics are the same coefficient,
and both sit below their fail bands.** SMI-01 is 0.29 of its fail band,
SMI-02 is 0.89 of its.

**WITHDRAWN: "a case-setup or mesh difference would not confine itself to
one coefficient."** The first version of this note reasoned from that,
and the movement is not confined to one coefficient. On both SMI cases
exactly two metrics moved, `CDo` and `CMy`; on PHY-05 all four did. The
inference that excluded case setup and mesh as causes therefore has no
premise here, and nothing in this note replaces it. Excluding those
causes needs the input-identity and boundary-localisation steps the
2026-07-21 triage ran, and neither was run.

## What is NOT established, stated because the shape invites assuming it

**THE 26.123 SIDE IS REPRODUCED. THE 26.122 SIDE IS NOT.** That
asymmetry is the whole of what is owed, and the first two versions of
this note had it wrong in both directions.

`pyfs-qa drift` executes its own physics run for each build, so
`DRF-26122-26123_2026-08-17_full` contains a 26.123 execution that is
INDEPENDENT of `PHY-26123_2026-08-17_full`. The two agree on all
thirty-eight metrics at full precision, seventeen-digit floats included.
That is the same instrument `TRI-SMI01-CMy_2026-07-21` used to exclude
noise, delivered for the B side without anyone planning it.

The A side has ONE execution, the drift run's own 26.122 leg. The
standalone `PHY-26122_2026-08-11` covers PHY-01 and PHY-02 only, and
neither carries an SMI case. So the rerun owed is 26.122's, not both.

**And the two "comparisons" were never two measurements.** The stored
references and the drift run's 26.122 values are identical on all eight
SMI metrics: `SMI-01` reference CL -0.0174, CDi 0.0030, CDo 0.1305, CMy
-1.7843, and `SMI-02` reference CL 12.1368, CDi 0.2244, CDo 0.4229, CMy
-116.6897, each equal to the corresponding `value_a`. Both comparisons
are the same subtraction, and calling them corroboration was wrong.

What that identity DOES say is worth stating in its place, because it is
the stronger fact: the references were seeded on 26.120 on 2026-07-21 and
the 26.122 run was made on 2026-08-17, and across two builds and
twenty-seven days not one of the eight SMI numbers moved. Only 26.123
departs.

**No cause is established.** With the confinement argument withdrawn,
what remains is a pattern consistent with a deterministic change in the
viscous drag model between builds 8092026 and 8112026, and consistent is
not the same as shown.

**The boundary-layer mapping is neither shown nor excluded.** `RPT-027`
measured a large change in integrated viscous drag across a proximity
gap on 26.123, which the vendor attributes to the boundary-layer model;
that report refuses both the attribution and any claim of improvement in
as many words, and its corroboration section observes only that the
drift run "agrees" in DIRECTION.

An earlier version of this paragraph went further and said RPT-027's
Result 2 "establishes" that the viscous drag coefficients move on cases
with no proximity gap anywhere in them, and therefore EXCLUDES the
mapping here. That is withdrawn, for the same reason as the confinement
argument above: it rests on a property nobody measured. RPT-027 measured
gaps on its own two-wing article and on no SMI file, and SMI-02 is
`31_WBH_IH0`, a wing-body-tail assembly, which is the configuration
class the vendor's caveat is about. Nothing in this repository records
whether either saved simulation carries a proximal-boundary marking.
Measuring it, as a minimum-gap figure per file with no geometry
committed, is what would settle the exclusion; until then a reader must
not take the corroboration section as a disposition, and must not take
this note as ruling the mapping out either.

## Decision (the author's, taken 2026-08-18)

**The references stay untouched and the WARNs stand.** No band is
widened and no reference is re-seeded on 26.123.

The decision was taken on the first version of this note and is
unchanged by the amendment, because it was the least-committing of the
three options on offer and the amendment makes the case for the other two
weaker rather than stronger: with no cause established, re-seeding would
assert one.

The stored references encode 26.120 behaviour deliberately, and a WARN
against them is the drift class doing its work rather than failing at it,
which is the same conclusion `TRI-SMI01-CMy_2026-07-21` reached about a
different coefficient on a different build pair.

## What this leaves open

**The 26.122 rerun** is owed the next time a licensed seat is spent on
these builds, and is registered as
`PLN-20260818-0200-cdo-rerun-owed-on-26123` and in the licensed evidence
queue on the roadmap page. The 26.123 side is already reproduced, so the
rerun is half the size the first two versions of this note implied.

**And the proximity measurement**, if the exclusion above is ever to be
made rather than withdrawn: a minimum-gap figure per SMI file and a
statement of whether either saved simulation carries a proximal-boundary
marking. Both are aggregates and neither commits geometry.

**And a question the withdrawn claim had hidden, which belongs to the
numerical-analyst seat.** SMI-01's `CMy` moved by 0.70 of its warn band
on this build pair, and `CMy` on SMI-01 is precisely the coefficient
`TRI-SMI01-CMy_2026-07-21` was written about, on the 26.101 against
26.120 pair, where it moved 0.8 percent and was dispositioned as a
deterministic solver-side change. It has now moved again, on a different
pair, in the opposite direction, at seventy percent of the band that
would flag it. Whether a viscous-model-only reading is still the one the
author wants on the record is hers, and the rerun above is the
measurement that would settle it.
