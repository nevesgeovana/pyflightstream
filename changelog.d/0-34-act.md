## Added

- The plan warns, and never refuses, on a row whose actuator disc has `wake_type = "RELAXED"` and names a loading `PROFILE`, naming the row, the disc and RPT-137: on 26.124 a RELAXED disc ignored a custom profile and carried about half of the thrust asked. The plan's statuses and the plan file are what they would be without the warning (FR-332).
- The documentation states four actuator-disc behaviours measured on 26.124 and summarised in RPT-137: the probe velocities of a quasi-steady run are in the rotating frame of the blade (probes and quasi-steady pages); the disc's swirl is one global factor, not a radial distribution; the ELLIPTICAL model placed 0.62 of the thrust asked in the wake; a RELAXED disc ignored a custom profile and carried about half of the thrust (actuator page). The last two are measured values, not guaranteed ones (FR-332).
- RPT-137, the summary of the measured research study of the actuator disc on 26.124 (build 8172026): the swirl sense, the swirl factor, the thrust ELLIPTICAL and RELAXED put in the wake, the quasi-steady probe frame and the default wake end plane of a blades-only wheel; nondimensional, no new run (FR-331, FR-332).

## Changed

- An actuator disc whose reference block states `rpm_sign = 1` now swirls its wake the way a rotor of `rpm_sign = 1` turns: the script hands `SET_PROP_ACTUATOR_RPM` minus the block's sign times the row's speed, where 0.33.0 handed plus, the sense measured on 26.124 to swirl with the rotor (RPT-137). The emitted disc speed line of every disc changes sign and nothing else changes; `scripts/check_parity.py` names the difference under FR-331 (FR-331).

## Migration

- **The actuator disc's swirl sense is reversed (FR-331).** From 0.34.0 a disc whose block states `rpm_sign = 1` swirls its wake the way a rotor of `rpm_sign = 1` turns, as the block's docstring always said; up to 0.33.0 it swirled the other way, because the script handed the solver plus the sign times the speed and 26.124 turns a disc handed plus against the rotor of the same sign (RPT-137). Every emitted `SET_PROP_ACTUATOR_RPM` line carries the opposite sign of the line 0.33.0 wrote for the same row. A study that set `rpm_sign = -1` on a disc to make it swirl with its rotor sets `rpm_sign = 1` from 0.34.0, the rotor's own hand; a study that kept `rpm_sign = 1` and read its disc wake as the rotor's now gets the swirl the rotor gives. A disc result recorded by 0.33.0 or earlier keeps the sense it was run with: its run record holds the script it ran. The sense is measured on 26.124 only; on another build the same rule is applied and unmeasured.
