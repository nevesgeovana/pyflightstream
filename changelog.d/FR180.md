## Fixed

- The per-revolution drift warning (FR-180) no longer fires on force and moment components that are near zero. A force or moment column is now judged against its scale, the largest previous-revolution mean of the same kind (force, moment or force coefficient) in its plot group, so an in-plane force is judged against the thrust and an in-plane moment against the torque: it warns when its last-revolution change exceeds `drift_limit_pct` per cent of that scale. The column that sets the scale warns exactly as before. A rotor in uniform inflow, whose in-plane loads are hundredths of a newton, previously filled `post.log` with a warning per component per point. The warning line now states the change against the scale; the `_DRIFT_PCT` columns of the table keep the relative drift and are byte-identical.
