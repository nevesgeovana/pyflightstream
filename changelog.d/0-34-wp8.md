## Changed

- The models of `pyflightstream.cases` live in six public modules (AD-16):
  `pyflightstream.cases.pproc` (the exports vocabulary and the pproc artifact),
  `pyflightstream.cases.reference_blocks` (the rotor, actuator and blade blocks
  of a reference, `CampaignConfigError` and `AliasCycleError`),
  `pyflightstream.cases.settings` (`SolverSettings`, `FluidState`, `PointState`,
  `point_state_key` and `ReferenceData`), `pyflightstream.cases.mesh` (the mesh
  import and the mesh-side boundary models), `pyflightstream.cases.naming` (the
  point and sweep names) and `pyflightstream.cases.selection` (the family and
  alias selection). Every name keeps its `pyflightstream.cases` path, where it is
  the same object, and the root's `__all__` is unchanged in content and order;
  the six are type-checked (the exemption list did not grow) and each has its
  page in the API reference. Nothing a campaign, a script or a product holds
  changes.
