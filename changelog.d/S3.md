## Added
- `EXPORT_UNSTEADY_LAST_REV: <turns>` (a row of `unsteady_rotor`) and `EXPORT_UNSTEADY_LAST_ITER: <steps>` (a row of `unsteady` or `unsteady_rotor`) make the per-step exports cover the last revolutions or the last steps of the run, from step `TIME_ITERATIONS - n + 1` to the last, with `n = ceil(turns x steps per revolution)` or `n = <steps>`; the script, counter program and per-step files are those of `EXPORT_UNSTEADY_AFTER_ITER` at that first step (FR-415).
- The run record's `export_window` states the new forms as `last_revolutions` and `last_iterations` beside `iterations` and `revolutions`, with the value as written and the resolved `first_step` (FR-415).

## Changed
- A row stating two of the four per-step export keys (`EXPORT_UNSTEADY_AFTER_REV`, `EXPORT_UNSTEADY_AFTER_ITER`, `EXPORT_UNSTEADY_LAST_REV`, `EXPORT_UNSTEADY_LAST_ITER`) is refused at plan naming the two it states; a last count of zero, a negative one, or one beyond `TIME_ITERATIONS` is refused naming the run length (FR-415).
- With a pproc `[time_averaging]`, a last form whose first step falls after the averaging window's first step is refused as the after-threshold forms are (FR-415).

## Migration
- Nothing to change: a row stating none of the four keys renders byte-identical to 0.36.0 (FR-415).
