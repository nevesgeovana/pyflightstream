The lifting-surface sections of a geometry configuration can be given as CST coefficients (`cst_upper`, `cst_lower`, `te`) or as a file of x, z ordinates (`file`, read from the folder of the input), beside a NACA designation; a section is held behind one `SectionForm` interface with CST as its stored form.

An identified wing with dihedral now rebuilds on the symmetry plane and to the tip end plane: the root is no longer lowered to the lowest sample along the tilted span axis, and the tip is extended to the end plane the leading edge reaches.
