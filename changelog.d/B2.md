## Added

- `pyfs-matrix sync` names every `sims/sim_*` folder of both workspaces, recorded or not: how many each side holds, which are only in one, and every folder no record of the merged manifest carries (FR-220; [Sync every folder, and the two matrix homes](docs/sync-folders-and-matrix-homes.md)).
- `pyfs-matrix sync --restore --apply` rebuilds the records of those folders through `pyfs-matrix rebuild` once the sync has released the `runs.json` lease; a refused rebuild is written in the entry and the copied files stand (FR-221).
- `pyfs-matrix sync --include-archives` brings the files under folders named `archive` (FR-223).
- `sync`, `free-space` and `delete-sims` take `--runs NAME`: `sync` merges into that manifest of main, `delete-sims` edits it and archives it first, `free-space` reads it in addition to `runs.json` (FR-226).
- `pyflightstream.workspace.matrix_by_stem` and `find_matrix`: one matrix per stem across the workspace root and `inputs/matrices/` (FR-224).
- The sync reports its `sync hash`, `sync merge` and `sync copy` stages to the stage progress (FR-227).

## Changed

- The sync copies each file and matrix to a temporary name in the target's folder and renames it in place once its digest matches, so an interrupted sync never leaves a partial file, and an overwritten file stays in place until its replacement is whole (FR-222).
- The sync holds the `runs.json` lease for the whole of its merge and copy, where it held it around the merge only; a restore, a run, a collect or a second sync refuses or waits while it writes (FR-225).
- The plan's POL census and the post find a matrix in `inputs/matrices/` as at the root; the post's warning for a matrix it cannot use names both homes (FR-224).

## Migration

- `pyfs-matrix sync` no longer copies archive folders: every file under a folder named `archive` (`post/<matrix>/**/archive/<stamp>/`, an `archive/` inside a simulation) is skipped and counted in the preview. Add `--include-archives` to bring them as before (FR-223).
- One matrix stem in both the workspace root and `inputs/matrices/` is read once when the two files are identical, where `sync` refused it and the plan refused every POL of it; with different content `sync` and `plan` refuse naming both paths, and `post` warns naming both and falls back to the run records. Keep one copy, or make the two identical (FR-224).
- `sync --restore` is off by default: a sync never writes a record it did not merge unless asked (FR-221).
