## Added

- `pyfs-matrix post --runs NAME` and `pyfs-matrix collect --runs NAME` read the run records of the manifest `NAME` in the workspace root, and `runs.json` is neither read nor written; the collect completes that manifest's submitted records under its own lock, and a named manifest that is not there is refused by its name (FR-230; library: `pyflightstream.run.records.manifest_workspace`).
- The products of a post of other records go to their own folder, `post/<matrix>@<stem>/` for `--runs NAME` and `post/<matrix>@sims/` for `--from-sims`, with their own `products.json`, `post.log`, `archive/` and `reports/`, so the default products are never overwritten and the two posts can be compared (FR-231).
- `pyfs-matrix post MATRIX --from-sims` posts points run outside the package: the records are assembled in memory from the loads exports under `sims/sim_<POL>/`, the row, its reference and its setup, and what cannot be recovered (the reference and aliases, the flight condition, the point of the sweep, the averaging window, the steps per revolution, which `--steps-per-revolution N` states) is refused by name, printed and written into `post.log`, never guessed (FR-232; library: `pyflightstream.run.records.assemble_records`, `from_sims_workspace`).

## Changed

- `post` and `collect` report their stage to the progress interface of 0.32.0 (FR-230).
- The post stage writes its measurement reports under `CampaignWorkspace.reports_root(matrix)`, the root for `runs.json`, so a default post writes exactly where it did (FR-231).

## Migration

- `post` and `collect` take `--runs NAME`, which 0.31.0 did not have, to read another manifest of the workspace root; the products of such a post are in `post/<matrix>@<stem>/`, beside `post/<matrix>/`, which it never writes (FR-230, FR-231).
- A post from the simulation folders is `pyfs-matrix post <matrix> --from-sims`, its products in `post/<matrix>@sims/` under the point names and the `<code>+sweep` token the records were assembled with; a steady point with no log export reads `FAILED_INCOMPLETE_OUTPUT` there and is still posted with a warning, and a record assembled this way has no rotor block, so its rotor tables are named skips (FR-232).
