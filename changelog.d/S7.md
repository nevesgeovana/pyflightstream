## Added

- `pyfs-matrix post` writes the solver settings of every recorded point as one numeric table per matrix, `settings/<matrix>_settings.csv` (the wide form, one row per point), opening with the key columns `POL` and `RUN_ID`, and its codebook `settings/<matrix>_settings.codebook.json`; both are listed in `products.json` with kind `settings_codebook`, and a point whose record holds no solver-setup snapshot is named in `skipped` and in `post.log` instead of getting a blank row (FR-419).
- `[products] settings_codebook` in the pproc, true by default; `false` turns the product off (FR-419).
- `write_settings_table` takes `legend=` (where the codebook is written) and `keys=` (text columns that open each row of the wide form, such as `POL` and `RUN_ID`); `read_settings_table` returns such columns as text (FR-419).

## Changed

- The default products of a campaign now include the settings table and its codebook, by the owner's decision of 2026-10-05: with `settings_codebook = false` the products are those of 0.36.0 byte for byte (FR-419).

## Migration

- A workspace posted with 0.36.0 gets a `settings/` folder, two entries in `products.json` and, for every point whose record predates the solver-setup snapshot, one WARNING line in `post.log` naming it. State `[products] settings_codebook = false` in the pproc to keep the products as they were. A post under `--strict` exits 3 for such a workspace, since each of those points is a recorded skip; the same key stops it.
