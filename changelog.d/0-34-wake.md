## Added

- The setup key `wake_termination_length` states a rotor row's wake termination as a length of wake in rotor radii; the rotor builder converts it into the steps `SET_WAKE_TERMINATION_TIME_STEPS` takes, `n = ceil(L R Omega / (V_ax dtheta))`, with V_ax the free-stream speed and R half the largest rotor diameter the row turns, rounded up so the wake kept is never shorter than L. The run record's solver snapshot names the L asked, the V_ax used with its rule and the steps emitted, and the plan states the same values for each rotor point; for a step or revolution count the plan states the length of wake it keeps (FR-321, FR-325).
- Near hover, `wake_termination_thrust_n` (a thrust in newtons) convects a wake length at the momentum-theory induced velocity `sqrt(T / (2 rho A))` where it exceeds the free-stream speed, and `wake_termination_revolutions_cap` bounds the converted steps; the record names the rule used (`free_stream`, `induced_velocity` or `revolution_cap`). A rotor row at zero free-stream speed stating neither is refused at plan, naming both keys (FR-323).
- The setup key `wake_termination_x_m` places the solver's wake end plane: the `WAKE_TERMINATION_X` argument of `INITIALIZE_SOLVER`, `DEFAULT` or an X in metres in the simulation's reference frame. A number with a unit, a distance in radii, a non-finite number, an empty value and any other word are refused at plan; a setup that does not state the key writes `DEFAULT`, as 0.33.0 did (FR-324).
- The plan warns, and never refuses, on every rotor point whose wake may not reach its length: a length (stated, or the 4R default) the run's revolutions cannot reach; a step or revolution count that keeps less than the 4R recommendation; a stated wake end plane before the length, or one the plan cannot place because the rotor's hub is unknown; and the solver's `DEFAULT` plane, whose position the plan cannot know, citing the two placements measured on 26.124 (5.5 R on a rotor case, 2.1 R on a blades-only wheel). The plan's statuses, exit status and file are what they would be without the warnings (FR-325).

## Changed

- At most one of `wake_termination_length`, `wake_termination_steps` and `wake_termination_revolutions` states a row's wake termination: two reaching one row, both from its setup preset, one from the preset and one from the row's `VAR_NAMES_VALUES` cell, or both from the row, are refused at plan, naming each key, its value and the file or column it comes from. The refusal of 0.33.0 for a preset stating revolutions beside steps is now this rule (FR-322).
- A rotor row of `unsteady_rotor` that states no wake termination keeps a wake of 4 rotor radii: its script gains one `SET_WAKE_TERMINATION_TIME_STEPS` line, the 4R default converted into steps, and nothing else changes. `scripts/check_parity.py` names the line under FR-321 (FR-321).

## Migration

- **A rotor row that states no wake termination now keeps 4 rotor radii of wake (FR-321).** From 0.34.0 the script of an `unsteady_rotor` row whose setup and row state none of `wake_termination_length`, `wake_termination_steps` and `wake_termination_revolutions` carries one `SET_WAKE_TERMINATION_TIME_STEPS` line, the 4R default converted into steps from the free-stream speed, the rotor speed and the step angle; 0.33.0 wrote no termination line, so the wake was bounded only by the run's length and the solver's default end plane (FR-324). A row that wants the 0.33.0 script states its termination. A rotor row at zero free-stream speed that states no termination is refused at plan until it states `wake_termination_thrust_n` or `wake_termination_revolutions_cap` (FR-323), and two termination keys on one row are refused (FR-322). A result recorded by 0.33.0 or earlier keeps the script it ran in its run record.
