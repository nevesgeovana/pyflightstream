## Added

- The post stage reads the solver's acoustic export (`pyflightstream.post.acoustics.read_acoustic_signals`) into one signal per observer (FR-260).
- Per observer, the pressure against time and the one-sided spectrum, written under `acoustics/` of the products (FR-261).
- The overall sound pressure level of each observer, `20 log10(p_rms / 20e-6 Pa)`, in `<point>_acoustics_summary.csv` (FR-262).
- The blade-passage harmonics of each observer from the rotor's blades and speed, `NA` with a `post.log` line where the record has neither (FR-263).
- The directivity (angle and OASPL) when four or more observers lie on a circular arc, and the post-stage hook for a record that lists `acoustic_signals` (FR-264).
