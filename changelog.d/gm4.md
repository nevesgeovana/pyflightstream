A body is now one skinned surface cut at its stations by knot insertion, not a surface per bay: the pieces are the same surface as the whole, so the tangent plane is continuous across every cut. New `geometry/_bspline.py` (knot insertion, patch split), `_skin.py` and `_continuity.py`, a jump audit of every shared edge that reports the turn per edge kind and passes under 1 degree, or up to a declared crease. Body patches carry a `role` and the names of their four edges, so a mesher or an assembly reads them instead of list order. Bodies gain a boat-tail rear (`rear.boat_tail`, a closure law limited in slope), a `rings_file` of measured sections (CSV with x, width, height), a whole body about its axis (`symmetry = "full"`, azimuths 0 to 360, off-centre sections allowed) and open ends (`nose = "open"`, `rear = "open"`) whose rim a parent and a child can share. A cap quadrilateral and a band bay are sampled with a count that grows until the pole seams turn by at most 0.8 degrees. migration: a body's patches are now the skinned form, so a body mesh or IGES of 0.56.0 differs from this version by the interpolation error of the old per-bay form, and the research reference comparison of bodies is retired; a `rings_file` that is not a rings table warns and is skipped; a `rear` of `open` and the keys `nose` and `symmetry` are new.
