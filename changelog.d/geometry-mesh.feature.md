Added `mesh_generated`, the all-quad surface mesh of a generated parametric configuration: structured patches meshed from their role (spanwise constant width with a refined tip, chordwise growth from the leading edge, constant azimuth and axial step on a body band), a planar all-quad flat tip cap, the wing-fuselage transition ring as the only triangles, orientation outward, the acceptance checks (inverted, degenerate, non-manifold, free edges off the symmetry plane, distance to the source) and OBJ output with one group per component. The spacing laws, rules and checks are pure numpy in `pyflightstream.geometry._mesh_rules` and `_mesh_topology`; the meshing needs the optional `gmsh` extra and refuses with the install command when it is absent. Equal to the research mesher node for node on a comparison test (tier 2).
