## Added

- `pyfs-workspace profile sections|uni|bp` writes the radial thrust profile a row's `PROFILE: <stem>` names, `inputs/profiles/<stem>.csv`, beside a provenance record `<stem>.provenance.json`, the way the field operations write a custom free stream: from a POL's written sections (the sectional loads table a post wrote, found by `--pol` or named, `-Fx` at the stations, a zero from the axis to the hub and a zero at the tip, a table of several steps averaged over its last `--last K`), or as the uniform pressure jump (`F = c r`) or the Betz-Prandtl shape (Prandtl's tip and hub factors, `tan(phi) = J R / (pi r)`). Each is scaled so that `B * integral(F dr)` equals `--thrust` in N or `--ct` with `--rho` and `--rpm` (never both), the integral of the written rows stated in the record; the file is in the form RPT-070 measured (rows `r,F`, m and N/m per blade, no header, no final newline); it previews until `--apply` and never overwrites without `--overwrite`. The disc is a reference's actuator block (`--ref`, `--disc`) or stated. The ELLIPTICAL disc is native in the solver and needs no file; a RELAXED disc is noted, and the plan's warning on it is kept (FR-347).
- `pyflightstream.workspace.actuator_profiles`, the functions behind the command, and the page "Actuator-disc profiles" (`docs/actuator-profiles.md`), linked from the actuator-disc section of the row's flow inputs page (FR-347).
