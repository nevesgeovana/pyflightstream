## Added

- FR-265: the row key `ACOUSTIC_SOURCES: ENABLE` (or `DISABLE`, the control) of an `unsteady` or `unsteady_rotor` row switches the solver's acoustic sources on in the setup, before the solver initialises; see [acoustic signals](docs/acoustic-emission.md).
- FR-266: observers declared by the row as named points (`ACOUSTIC_OBSERVERS`, metres in the reference coordinate system), imported from `inputs/acoustics/<stem>.csv` (`ACOUSTIC_OBSERVERS_FILE`, the point's hashed copy), or as one annular grid (`ACOUSTIC_SECTION`), over the window `ACOUSTIC_OBSERVER_TIME: T0 T1 N`.
- FR-267: a row declaring observers computes the signals after the solve and exports them to `<point>_acoustic_signals.txt`, then writes the section's VTK files into `<point>_acoustic_section/`.
- FR-268: the signals file is a declared output, collected and hashed in the record like the point's other outputs, and every file of the section folder is listed and hashed too; none is ever read as a surface export or a loads table; a file already in the section folder before the run is refused as a leftover.
- FR-269: the acoustic setup after `INITIALIZE_SOLVER`, the computation or exports on a steady run or before the solve, an acoustic key on a steady row, and a continuation of a row stating acoustic keys are refused.
- FR-266: six acoustic commands are `verified` on 26.124 from the licensed round-1 probes (`reports/compat/CMP-26124_2026-09-30_acoustics.yaml`); `COMPUTE_ACOUSTIC_SIGNALS` stays `documented`, accepted with its effect not isolated.

## Migration

- FR-268: a record written by 0.32.0 may list files under `<point>_acoustic_section/` among its outputs, with their sha256, and a reader of `outputs` meets them as files of a subfolder of the datapoint folder; `classify_outputs` leaves them and `<point>_acoustic_signals.txt` out of every export kind.
