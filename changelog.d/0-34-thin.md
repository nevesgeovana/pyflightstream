## Added

- The thin blade of a blade mesh (FR-330, its library core): a private module beside the geometry clean, `pyflightstream.workspace._degenerate`, derives the mean surface of a blade from its saved simulation (`.fsm`) or OBJ, the surface midway between its two sides from the root to the tip, with its root moved outward along the span by a required root offset so it does not cross the spinner. It writes `<stem>_thin_blade.obj` beside the source and its boundary inventory `<stem>_thin_blade.boundaries.toml`, which states the root offset used and, where the source states it, the `[import]` length unit; it never modifies the source, refuses an existing output unless asked to overwrite, and refuses by name, writing nothing, a mesh it cannot read, a file holding more than one boundary, and a blade whose two sides it cannot separate. The command `pyfs-matrix degenerate` that calls it lands with the command-line family of the parser.
