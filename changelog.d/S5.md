## Added

- A pproc `[[probes]]` entry states its probe kind on `unsteady` and `unsteady_rotor` rows: `kind = "unsteady"` (the default, one fluid plot per point and parameter at every time step) or `kind = "normal"`, probe points created after the time march with the steady commands, updated and exported once to `<point>_probes.txt`, a declared output of the row; the probes table of a normal entry holds the run's last time step in `STEP` (FR-417).
- A normal `[[probes]]` entry with `reusable_inflow = true` or `field_formats` on an unsteady row writes the field products a steady row writes from the same probe values, `<point>_field_NN_step_<N>.inflow.dat` included, for the run's last time step; nothing is averaged over time (FR-418).

## Changed

- An unsteady row whose pproc mixes normal and unsteady probe entries (an entry with no `kind`, and a `[volume_section]`, counting as unsteady) is refused when the row is built, naming the entries of each kind; any other `kind` value is refused when the pproc is read (FR-417).
