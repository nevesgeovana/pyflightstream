## Fixed

- `pyfs-matrix plan` (and `plan_matrix`, `plan_campaign`) now warns when a section distribution of a pproc declares a family that the geometry of some row does not carry: one warning per pproc artifact and per missing family, naming the entry and every row that lacks it, and never a refusal, so every row plans READY as before and no emitted script changes. A numbered name counts by itself, so `Blade2` is named on a sector row whose mesh carries `Blade1` alone, and a misspelled family beside a good one is named the same way; until 0.32.0 both were skipped in silence whenever the entry still emitted something. `--ignore-missing-families false` refuses what it refused before (FR-320; [Post-processing artifact](docs/pproc-artifact.md)).
