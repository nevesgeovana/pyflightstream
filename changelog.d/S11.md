## Changed

- The super content states each rotor's diameter: the steady and quasi-steady super files and the unsteady polar (`polars/P<sim>_<name>_uns_avg.csv`) carry `DIAMETER_<alias>`, in metres, right after `RPM_<alias>`, the `diameter_m` of the rotor block the row's reference declares and the number the rotor table divides by; `NA` where the reference declares no block of that alias. A row that turns no rotor gains no column; in a campaign whose super file has the column, that row reads `NA` (FR-89).
- A `qsteady_rotor` point's speed, which its plan does not state, is taken from its quasi-steady record, the source its rotor table already read: its super-file row gains `RPM_<alias>` beside `DIAMETER_<alias>`, and `RPM_CLOCK` and `J_CLOCK` are filled in every product of the point that states the condition (the polar, the rotor table, the sections and disc tables, the super file), where they read `NA` (FR-89).

## Migration

- A script that reads the super file or the unsteady polar by column position must read by name: `DIAMETER_<alias>` is inserted after each `RPM_<alias>`, and on a quasi-steady point `RPM_<alias>` is new too. Every other column keeps its name and its order (FR-89).
