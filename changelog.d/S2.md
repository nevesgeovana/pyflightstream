## Added
- `RUN_WAKE_LENGTH_R: <L>` on an `unsteady_rotor` row states the run length as a target wake length in rotor radii, with exactly one of `DELTA_THETA` or `DELTA_TIME`: `TIME_ITERATIONS = ceil(L R Omega / (V_ax dtheta))`, by the conversion and the axial velocity rule (free stream, or the induced velocity of the setup's `wake_termination_thrust_n`) that size `wake_termination_length`. The script, the averaging window and every export threshold read the resolved count (FR-422, FR-415).
- `pyfs-matrix plan` prints the resolved step count, the revolutions and V_ax with its rule per point of such a row, and `plan.json` carries them as `run_wake_length`; the run record's solver-flag snapshot carries `run_wake_length`, `run_wake_rule`, `run_wake_v_ax_m_s` and `run_wake_time_iterations` (FR-422).

## Migration
- Nothing to change: a row without `RUN_WAKE_LENGTH_R` renders byte-identical to 0.36.0, and its `plan.json` points carry no new key (FR-422).
