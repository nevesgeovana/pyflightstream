The geometry package builds a `fillet` between a lifting surface and a body or a blister, and a `blister` under a body, and `generate` writes both. The fillet follows the line where the lifting surface meets its host, each section a cubic blend tangent to both and set by the laws `radius`, `handle` and `setback` (one value or a list along the spine; the radius defaults to 0.05 of the root chord); its base surface runs past both contact lines by `host_band` and `wing_band`, and its lower and upper faces are trims of that base, the lower one from the trailing edge unless `extend_lower_to_te = false`, which starts it at the thickest point. A closing face shuts the fillet at the trailing edge against the wing's blunt trailing-edge face and the host. The lifting surface may be rolled, so a fin fillet builds on both sides of a fin. On a blister the fillet lies on the blister's face, outside the body the blister lies on. The blister is a loft of sections in planes of constant x, superellipses or the sections of a `sections_file` (CSV columns `station`, `y`, `z` in metres whatever `[units]` declares, which a configuration with another length unit is warned about; keel to a point inside the host), closed in a pole at each end, split at its deepest station into a front and a rear base that share one edge, each running into the host and trimmed where it leaves it; `profile.depth` may be given per station, and `of = "host_length"` reads stations and depth as ratios of the host length. A `pyfs-geom` run builds each piece in its own sub-steps of the stage table, `S8.F1` to `S8.F3` for a fillet and `S8.B1` to `S8.B3` for a blister (build, trim by host, closure), each writing its record with the piece's inputs and stopping the run at its first failed check; a piece whose build cannot go on (hosts that do not meet, a blister section that does not enter its host) fails the run at the sub-step whose check that is, with the value measured. The checks are `fillet.spine`, `closure.tangent_host`, `closure.tangent_wing`, `closure.contact_gap_host`, `closure.contact_gap_wing`, `closure.base_present`, `fillet.host_footprint`, `fillet.contact_on_face`, `closure.free_edges`, `section.residual`, `continuity.bluff_jump`, `blister.crease_on_host`, `trim.closed`, `trim.in_domain` and `trim.area_removed`. The keys `mesh_zone` and `spine` of a fillet and `profile.plateau` and `profile.mirror_x` of a blister, which this version does not apply, are reported in the warnings and skipped, as are a blister's width, exponent and depth when a sections file gives its shape. A blister with fewer than three stations is warned about when the configuration is resolved and refused when it is built.

migration: a configuration with a fillet or a blister component, refused by `generate` before, now generates them.
