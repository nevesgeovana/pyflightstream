The geometry package builds a `fillet` between a lifting surface and its body host and a `blister` under a body, and `generate` writes both. The fillet follows the line where the lifting surface meets the body, each section a cubic blend tangent to both hosts and set by the laws `radius`, `handle` and `setback` (one value or a list along the spine; the radius defaults to 0.05 of the root chord); its base surface runs past both contact lines by `host_band` and `wing_band`, and its lower and upper faces are trims of that base, the lower one from the trailing edge unless `extend_lower_to_te = false`, which starts it at the thickest point. The blister is a loft of superellipse sections in planes of constant x, closed in a pole at each end, split at its deepest station into a front and a rear base that share one edge, each running into the host and trimmed where it leaves it; `profile.depth` may be given per station, and `of = "host_length"` reads stations and depth as ratios of the host length. The stage records add `fillet.spine`, `closure.base_present`, `closure.tangent_host`, `closure.tangent_wing`, `closure.contact_gap_host`, `closure.contact_gap_wing`, `section.residual`, `blister.crease_on_host` and `continuity.bluff_jump` across the blister's seam. The keys `mesh_zone` and `spine` of a fillet and `profile.plateau`, `profile.mirror_x` and `sections_file` of a blister, which this version does not apply, are reported in the warnings and skipped.

migration: a configuration with a fillet or a blister component, refused by `generate` before, now generates them.
